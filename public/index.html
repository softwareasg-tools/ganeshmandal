VEDIC TRADITION • STEP-BY-STEP VIDHI

Traditional Ganesh Chaturthi Puja Vidhi Guide

A complete devotee walkthrough of the authentic Pranapratishtha and Shodashopachara rituals for welcoming Bappa at home.

01
Purification & Deepa Puja (शुद्धीकरण व दीपपूजन)
Sprinkle Ganga jal on the wooden chowki (pat) and puja samagri. Light oil lamps chanting "Deepajyoti Parabrahma..." to invite positive energy.
02
Pranapratishtha Mantra (प्राणप्रतिष्ठा)
Hold akshata (sacred rice) and touch Bappa's heart with Durva blades chanting the Vedic Pranapratishtha Suktam: "ॐ गणानां त्वा गणपतिं हवामहे..."
03
Shodashopachara Puja (षोडशोपचार पूजा — 16 Sacred Offerings)
Offer Asana, Padya, Arghya, Snana (holy bath/panchamrita), Vastra (sacred thread & red cloth), Haldi-Kumkum, 21 blades of fresh green Durva grass, and red Hibiscus (Jaswand) flowers.
04
Naivedya & Maha Aarti (नैवेद्य व महाआरती)
Offer 21 freshly steamed Ukadiche Modaks, banana, and coconut naivedya. Recite "Sukhakarta Dukhaharta" and "Shendur Lal Chadhayo" accompanied by ghanta and shankh naad.
05
Eco-Friendly Visarjan Vidhi (उत्तरपूजा व विसर्जन)
Perform Uttarpuja with dhoop and flowers. Dissolve the natural Shadu clay idol in a water-filled tub or garden pot at home, returning earth to earth respectfully.

❓ When does Ganesh Chaturthi 2026 begin and what is the Visarjan date?
Ganesh Chaturthi 2026 begins on Monday, September 14, 2026. Anant Chaturdashi (the Grand Visarjan day) falls on Thursday, September 24, 2026.
❓ Who started the Sarvajanik Ganeshotsav in Maharashtra?
National leader Lokmanya Bal Gangadhar Tilak transformed private family Ganpati worship into a monumental public festival in Pune in 1893 (at Kesariwada and Bhausaheb Rangari Bhavan) to unite people against British colonial rule.
❓ Why should devotees use biodegradable Ganesh idols instead of PoP?
Plaster of Paris (PoP) idols take months to dissolve and poison rivers, lakes, and oceans with heavy metals and toxic paints. Natural Shadu clay dissolves in water within 2 hours without polluting drinking water or harming aquatic life.
